# Japanese Amateur Piano Competitions — Research Notes (Nov 2026)

Context: looking for an onsite (Japan) amateur piano competition, suitable for a
38-year-old, around early February 2027.

## Candidates matching early Feb 2027, onsite in Japan

### 全国大人のピアノコンクール (National Adult Piano Competition)
- Amateur division, age-bracketed (A/B/C...); 38 falls in the **B: 30代 (30s)** bracket
- Excludes conservatory graduates / current music instructors (master division is separate) — genuinely amateur-only
- **Chiba preliminary: Feb 6, 2027** — J:Com Urayasu Music Hall — entry deadline **Jan 7, 2027**
- Osaka preliminary: Feb 11, 2027 (Grace Cathedral) — deadline Jan 12, 2027; second Osaka venue Feb 21, 2027 — deadline Jan 22, 2027
- Entry fee: ~¥12,000–13,200 for a 6-minute preliminary performance

### ブルーメンシュタインピアノコンクール (Blumenstein Piano Competition)
- Open to adults 18+, piano enthusiasts; professionals excluded
- Levels from beginner to advanced; includes a no-score "チャレンジ部門" for those just wanting stage experience
- **Kanto II preliminary: Feb 6, 2027** — Marie Konzert, Itabashi, Tokyo — entry deadline **Jan 6, 2027**
- Entry fee: ¥13,000–19,000 depending on level (¥1,000 discount for returning participants)

Both land on **Feb 6, 2027** — good fit for "early Feb."

## Reputation comparison (how Japanese amateur pianists view these)

Source consensus (klavier-schule.com, largomusica.com, otomamire.com):

| | National Adult | Blumenstein | Elise Music Festival |
|---|---|---|---|
| Informal difficulty rating | ★★★★☆ | ★★★☆☆ | highest tier |
| Positioning | "serious challenge" (本格的に挑戦したい) | "a competition you learn from" (学べるコンクール) | the national benchmark |
| Field strength | Includes a Master division (conservatory grads / instructors) alongside amateur brackets → stronger overall field | Broad beginner–advanced range, supportive/close-knit | Very large participant pool, multi-city |
| Atmosphere | National scope, strict level divisions, objective evaluation | Close distance between organizers, judges, and contestants; detailed feedback (講評が丁寧) | Large-scale, higher-profile final |

**Typical amateur progression path** cited in these articles: start with Blumenstein →
move up to National Adult as skill improves → aim for Elise Music Festival as the
top national-level goal.

### Why Elise Music Festival (エリーゼ音楽祭) is seen as the most prestigious tier
- **National footprint**: preliminaries in 8 cities (Hokkaido, Fukuoka, Hiroshima,
  Kobe, Nagoya, Yokohama, Tokyo, Toride) feeding one national final — much larger
  geographic reach than National Adult or Blumenstein
- **Longevity & volume**: in its mid-teens as an annual event (15th/16th edition in
  2025/2026), consistently large entrant numbers — read as a proxy for legitimacy
- **Final venue**: national final held at Ginza Owl Hall (銀座王子ホール), Tokyo — a
  recognized recital venue, giving it a "real concert" feel vs. community halls/salons
  used by smaller competitions
- **Genre breadth**: accepts classical solo, jazz, popular, ensemble/four-hands, and
  even street-piano video entries — seen as covering the full range of "amateur piano"
  rather than just classical technique
- **Judging philosophy**: emphasizes musicality/expressiveness alongside technique,
  which amateur adult players see as a fairer, more holistic measure of their strengths
- Eligibility: open to amateurs 15+ (excluding junior-high-age students)
